Our Mission

This is a learning program for students of all levels to play games and learn about STEM while playing fun games. Each game has it's own touch where STEM is implemented but isn't focused on the STEM in order for the game to still be enjoyable for students. Many new generations of students are choosing to not pursue STEM as a career option because what they are learning in school which is STEM related is boring. This app uses a point system to encourage students to keep learning which can be used in the shop to donate money to real world issues, help marine life fight pollution, or help plant trees all across the world.

Points

After finishing each game by using problem solving skills, students are able to earn points which can be used in the shop. After earning a certain amount of points, students can use their points to help with real world problems by donating towards issues related to deforestation, marine life, and to support natural disaster survivors.
